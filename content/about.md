Hatchet Labs is an independent software studio. We build tools and ship
them early.

## How we work

- **Ship early.** Projects go live as betas on their own subdomain as soon as
  they're useful to someone.
- **Listen.** Betas exist to get feedback, and we read all of it.

## What you'll find here

The [projects](../projects/) page lists everything we're building, from early
experiments to things that have graduated to stable.
